## 2. Non-negotiable security boundaries

1. **Authentication identifies a person. Membership authorizes a workspace. An entitlement enables purchased features.** All three are separate records. A paid receipt never creates membership in someone else's organisation or enables founder tools.
   For an independent engineer's personal workspace, the grant is authenticated identity equal to its immutable personal-owner ID, with the normal role/verification/resource checks. Company membership and a team subscription are not required there. This never supplies a fallback for a denied organisation context. Include fresh independent-engineer success and foreign-personal-workspace denial tests.
2. Every private query, write, file, export, notification, realtime subscription, worker and administrative action derives authorization from verified identity plus current server-side membership and resource ownership. The client may request a workspace ID; the server verifies it. A hidden tab or signed JWT containing an old role is insufficient after removal or suspension.
3. Reject missing tenant ownership; never silently assign a legacy record to whichever account is currently signed in. Backfill from proven ownership or quarantine for review. Include tenant IDs in uniqueness constraints, caches, aggregate jobs and storage authorization, not only in UI filters.
4. An organisation's engineer can access only explicitly assigned/shared work. Joining the team does not expose their private independent jobs, other memberships, personal bank details or unrelated conversations. Transfers and reassignment require a recorded, permitted workflow.
5. Team administrators cannot confirm work on behalf of the hospital, change a hospital's invoice approval, self-approve their verification, create clinical credentials or mark an unpaid order paid. Sensitive dual-party steps preserve independent hospital acceptance.
6. Local drafts, pending payment proofs, outbox rows and notification envelopes carry `userId + loginGeneration + workspaceId + originatingOperationId` where applicable. Check ownership inside the serialized mutation and again before displaying/delivering. A stale callback cannot adopt the current login at setter entry.
7. Storage policy, SQL RLS, RPC/Edge authorization, server job credentials and export endpoints are separately tested. A service-role job must explicitly scope every operation; its RLS bypass must not become a customer-facing general query facility.
8. Unknown/failed authorization is not Engineer, paid, verified or founder. Show a bounded recovery state. Preserve useful local drafts without allowing offline membership grants, money release or unverified server writes.

### Offline access and honest revocation limits

Online endpoints reject a removed/suspended membership on the next request; this does not magically erase a disconnected phone. Routine offline reads may use a short-lived server-issued lease bound to user, login generation, workspace, capability version and expiry, with a **proposed maximum 24-hour lifetime**. Only explicitly permitted, minimally cached routine service data qualifies; no new privileged export, billing/admin action, role/assignment grant, evidence download or financial authorization is admitted offline. Sensitive verification/bank/owner data requires an online check. Every foreground/reconnect revalidates before resuming protected network work, and lease expiry locks protected cached views pending validation.

Use the last trusted server time with monotonic elapsed time within the same boot for expiry checks. A reboot, missing trusted time anchor or detected clock rollback makes the lease unusable until revalidation; wall-clock changes cannot extend it. An authenticated account/workspace switch retires the old lease immediately. Removing membership or a security suspension revokes server access regardless of subscription grace. Lease tests must prove the bounded offline window and local lock, not claim instant remote cache deletion. A person may already have viewed, photographed or exported authorized data; those copies cannot be recalled. Rooted-device local enforcement is not assumed tamper-proof, so minimize cached data and encrypt it with appropriately scoped keys.

### Founder and customer administrators

Founder oversight means operational control with accountability, not an unlogged impersonation capability. Use a separate privileged session, strong MFA, short sensitive-action reauthentication, least-privilege operator scopes and a private provisioning process. No founder registration button, email-domain shortcut, client role flag or paid upgrade grants this privilege.

Default founder views show service health, aggregate business data, verification queues, disputes and scoped audit records. Viewing a customer's private evidence requires a support case, purpose, permitted scope, short expiry and a visible audit event. Redact fields irrelevant to the case. Never ask a user for their password or OTP; do not mint an invisible customer session.

Refund approvals, payout-account changes, verification overrides, tenant ownership transfers and bulk exports use separate explicit actions. Require a second authorized approver for high-risk exceptions when staff exists. During single-founder operation, disable self-approved exceptional money releases/ownership overrides rather than pretend there are two independent approvers; use the documented provider/support escalation path. Maintain an emergency break-glass process with strong authentication, narrow scope, expiry, immediate alerts and subsequent review. It cannot silently alter settled money or erase its own audit history.

## 3. Registration, verification, membership and recovery

**Engineer:** verify contact ownership, collect professional name, State/UT, district, skills and service coverage. Qualification/experience and optional employer evidence are reviewed separately. A badge must say what was checked and when; do not imply a universal government biomedical-engineer licence. Payout activation uses the payment provider's required verification and verified beneficiary, not an uploaded screenshot alone.

**Hospital:** verify the person and separately their authority to represent the facility. Match the facility name/contact and corroborating establishment/authorisation documents or a callback through an independently sourced channel. A matching email domain alone is weak evidence. Prevent duplicate facility takeover; surface an existing facility's join/request-access path without leaking its staff list. A new manager needs an authorized invitation or reviewed transfer, not a self-declared title.

**Engineering organisation:** allow a legitimate sole proprietor/person as well as a company. Verify the owner and business identity proportionately; do not make GST/Aadhaar mandatory for everyone by assumption. Separate organisation verification, individual engineer verification and provider payout checks. Payment does not skip any verification state.

**Invites:** server-issued, high-entropy, hashed at rest, single-use, expiring and revocable; bound to workspace, intended verified identity/channel and proposed role. Acceptance is authenticated and atomic with membership/seat limits. Preview the organisation and role, require consent, and reject wrong-recipient, forwarded, expired and simultaneous duplicate acceptance. Revoking a member invalidates outstanding sessions/caches for that workspace and pending privileged operations. Replacing the last owner requires a verified successor and an auditable transfer; no accidental ownerless organisation.

**Recovery:** offer account-specific supported recovery for email/password and federated providers. Require recent authentication and verified control of existing channels for sensitive changes; a recovered session alone does not immediately change a payout beneficiary. Notify old and new verified channels of identity/owner changes, with a dispute path. Defend OTP/password attempts against enumeration and distributed abuse using bounded per-account, device and network limits with monitored escalation. Do not reveal whether a stranger's phone/email already belongs to a hospital.

**Location:** State/UT and district are structured, server-validated IDs from the versioned location catalogue. Engineers may explicitly choose additional coverage districts; facility and organisation offices each have their own district. No maps, location permission, GPS proof of arrival or location-based anti-fraud requirement. Show a textual service address/contact only to permitted parties. District selection is a service filter, not proof of identity or presence. District renames/splits keep historical references and reviewable mappings.

## 4. Demo, trial and subscription boundaries

The launch experience offers an **interactive demo with synthetic hospitals, engineers, jobs and money**, implemented only as client-local seeded fixtures with their own repository/data namespace. A server-backed demo is deferred and requires a separate product/security decision before implementation; any later proposal must prove non-production isolation, private per-demo namespaces, quotas and automatic reset. No shared real-account credentials and no anonymous access to the production database.

All demo operations remain simulated: no real OTP spam, emails, invites, FCM tray notifications, payouts, payment SDK checkout, production storage upload or live job posting. Mark every relevant screen and document as Demo. Only nonsensitive choices such as preferred language may carry across when the person explicitly creates a live account; demo records and receipts cannot be promoted to production.

A live trial is different and **is not part of the initial launch**. If later introduced, require verified identity, a real workspace with explicit acceptance of trial terms, limited seats/quotas and normal authorization. Prevent repeated trial creation using proportionate verified business/account evidence; a shared hospital network or device alone does not prove abuse. Keep an appeal path. Do not silently start a recurring charge after the demo or a future no-card trial.

Model organisation verification, subscription and risk suspension independently. The canonical **derived app-access states** are **Pending, Active, Grace, Restricted and Suspended**. Keep raw provider billing state, server-verified paid-through time, provider grace-end time, cancellation-at-period-end flag, purchase version and refund/revocation evidence in separate fields. A cancelled-at-period-end billing record can still derive Active until its verified paid-through time. A pending purchase does not unlock Active. Demo remains a separate mode, and live trial is deferred. Launch with one monthly team plan. The exact price, included active-seat limit, tax terms, cancellation/refund policy and provider contracts remain decisions before selling; this plan does not invent live charges.

Expiry restricts new premium operations rather than deleting data or holding active service work hostage. Permit the documented minimum safe completion of existing assignments, invoices, disputes, security settings, renewal and the user's permitted export/deletion request. Limit team growth/new premium jobs according to the agreed terms. Membership removal and fraud suspension remain independent security decisions. Do not let a revoked member use expiry grace to retain access.

### Access transition and precedence matrix

Hard authentication, current membership/resource authorization and security denials are evaluated **before** subscription access. Grace is allowed only from server-verified provider entitlement and its explicit deadline; a device clock, network error or manual retry cannot create or extend it. Restricted completion means the server-defined subset for an already committed job at its accepted scope, not new assignments, new quotes, paid upgrades or arbitrary new spending.

| Observed state / race | Derived decision and resolution |
|---|---|
| Unauthenticated, unverified required identity, removed member or wrong tenant, even with a paid receipt | Deny the protected request. Show only permitted recovery/support; billing cannot override identity/ownership. |
| Confirmed security suspension with active payment | Suspended; supervised handover/dispute/support as explicitly permitted. No self-reactivation by repurchase or recovery. |
| Verified purchase pending, or valid payment while required organisation review is pending | Pending; retain receipt/reference and support/refund path. Do not silently create workspace authority. |
| Verified membership and successful current purchase with future paid-through time | Active; only the purchased, authorized workspace capabilities. |
| Cancel-at-period-end while paid-through time remains | Active until authoritative paid-through time; show cancellation and renewal state accurately. |
| Renewal failure with provider-confirmed future grace end | Grace until that server-verified deadline, with provider-appropriate capability policy. No extra client-created grace. |
| Paid-through/grace elapsed; payment invalidated or authoritative entitlement revoked | Restricted for new paid commitments. Preserve allowed existing-work completion, receipts/disputes and data requests without granting former members access. |
| Provider/verification outage or offline client with old state | Do not invent Active/Grace or extend deadlines. Preserve the already verified bounded lease for allowed routine reads; retry/reconcile pending transactions without charging again. |
| Old paid webhook arrives after a later refund, revoke, replacement or expiry | Compare authoritative purchase/version/transition state; reject stale resurrection. Re-fetch provider status when ordering is ambiguous; reconciliation is idempotent. |
| Membership removed during checkout or completion attempt | No access/commit from the removed actor. Preserve valid payment evidence for the workspace and route recovery/refund to permitted billing authority; never change tenant ownership to make payment fit. |
| Two invitations/assignments compete for the last seat, or entitlement expires between check and write | Capacity and permitted entitlement checked atomically at the server mutation; exactly the permitted operation commits. |
| Delayed subscription callback arrives after account/workspace switch | Apply only to its server-recorded purchase/workspace; do not publish another workspace's data into current UI. |

The permission check remains authoritative even in Active/Grace. The app-access state is a presentation and capability projection, not a new super-role or standalone bearer credential.

## 5. Payment rail decision and transaction design

### Current distribution policy, checked 19 September 2026

**Team-management SaaS is a digital subscription.** Google Play's policy includes paid in-app functionality and business/cloud software; the default for a Play-distributed Android purchase is Play Billing unless an applicable exception/program is actually satisfied. Physical equipment-repair services use a separate service-payment rail; Play Billing must not be used for payments primarily for physical services. An onsite AMC may belong to the physical-service rail, but a bundled software-access subscription needs separate policy review. Do not relabel digital access as a repair fee. [Google Play payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en)

India's alternative-billing program permits an eligible, enrolled app to offer the alternative **alongside Play Billing** with required integration, user choice, transaction reporting, trust requirements and fees. The official integration path requires reporting authorized transactions within 24 hours. India does not provide a blanket Razorpay-only exception for this SaaS plan. For initial release, choose Play Billing for in-app team subscriptions; revisit the enrolled alternative only with an explicit implementation/business case. A website link, embedded webview, signup redirect or enterprise label is not automatically an exemption. Confirm any separately negotiated enterprise/consumption-only distribution model with current Play policy before using it. [Official India program](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13306652)

Subscription pricing must clearly show the billing period, seat/feature limits, taxes as applicable, renewal timing, trial end, cancellation route and what happens on expiry. Do not show a fabricated countdown, preselected paid upgrade, misleading free CTA or a charge before informed confirmation. Keep SaaS revenue and repair/AMC settlement in separate ledgers, receipts, reconciliation and refund flows.

### Server-owned money and entitlement

- The server creates the payable item and validates permitted buyer/workspace, product, quantity, currency and current price. The app never supplies an authoritative amount, beneficiary, paid flag or entitlement expiry.
- Bind each purchase to the intended permitted workspace and payer using a server record. Verify provider/Play evidence on the backend, including app/product/account mappings and authoritative state. Prevent a purchase token from unlocking two tenants. Pending means pending, not active. Record verified entitlement and acknowledge supported purchases promptly through durable, retryable server work; reconcile provider notifications and missed acknowledgments. [Play purchase-security guidance](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security)
- Purchase restoration must revalidate ownership and provider state, including replaced/linked purchases. A different EquipSeva account on the same Play account cannot silently steal a subscription; offer verified ownership recovery. Membership changes do not automatically transfer the billing contract.
- Signed webhook handling validates the exact provider contract, deduplicates immutable event IDs, authenticates the sender and fetches current provider state where required. Delivery can be duplicated, delayed or reordered. Provider webhooks update only a server-resolved purchase mapping; a client-supplied tenant ID cannot redirect them.
- Use integer minor currency units and explicit currency. Every credit, refund, adjustment and payout gets an append-only ledger entry, stable operation ID and database-enforced uniqueness/transition rule. A timeout is uncertain, not proof of failure. Check/reconcile before a new charge or payout. Preserve recoverable paid-but-uncredited proof across logout and restart in an owner-scoped encrypted store.
- Refunded/revoked subscriptions update future entitlement according to actual provider/legal terms, not a client callback. Repair refunds and SaaS cancellations are distinct. Default service refunds go back through the original payment provider/method; no manual different-person UPI payout to process a refund.
- For repair funds, use a suitable authorized provider and its supported marketplace/settlement contract. Do not build an informal EquipSeva wallet, promise legal escrow, hold pooled funds in a founder account or collect card/CVV/UPI PIN data. Money custody, merchant-of-record responsibility, KYC, invoicing/tax, disputes and payout liability must be agreed before live collection.
- Beneficiary changes require strong recent authentication, provider revalidation, alerts to the previous verified contact and a defined risk-based hold/review before use. Transfer requests snapshot beneficiary revision and amount. A later edit cannot redirect an in-flight payout. A team administrator cannot rewrite an independent engineer's personal settlement destination.

The RBI's 2025 Payment Aggregator direction covers authorization, merchant due diligence, settlement/escrow and dispute responsibilities for entities undertaking PA business. EquipSeva must confirm its role and provider arrangement; using a gateway SDK is not proof of compliance or permission to aggregate funds. Any recurring alternative-payment rail also needs its current provider/e-mandate requirements checked before launch. This plan does not claim regulatory certification. [RBI official direction, 15 September 2025; checked 19 September 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)

## 6. Root, tamper, clone and API protection

No distributed Android app can truthfully guarantee that it cannot be copied, rooted, reverse-engineered or hacked. An app cannot prevent a person rooting their own phone. The objective is to make copied/tampered clients unable to bypass server authorization, extract other customers' records or approve money, while detecting abuse and allowing legitimate users to recover.

Use Play App Signing/release-key discipline, HTTPS, release debugging restrictions, minimized exported components, verified allow-listed links, encrypted owner-scoped sensitive local storage, backup exclusions, dependency scanning, obfuscation and private symbol upload as layers. Do not ship service-role/provider/signing secrets in the APK. Client root/Frida/installer/certificate heuristics may raise risk, but can be patched. Play Integrity is also one signal, not proof that an action is authorized. [Google's recommended layered/tiered approach](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/overview)

For protected actions, move enforcement to the actual backend mutation:

1. Use standard requests with a canonical digest of all relevant action inputs, including operation ID, authenticated actor/login context, workspace, resource revision and the intended action. Validate the digest and request freshness on the server. If classic requests remain, issue an expiring server challenge and atomically consume it against the exact operation; a client-generated random nonce alone is inadequate.
2. Verify expected package, recognized signing/version policy and the chosen licensing/device verdict. Missing/malformed/unevaluated fields are not a clean pass. Maintain signing rotation/minimum-version rollout rules so legitimate staged releases are not accidentally locked out. Google describes automatic replay mitigation for standard requests; it does not replace application-level idempotency or action authorization. [Standard request/content binding](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/standard), [verdict fields and freshness](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/verdicts)
3. Consume a short-lived server authorization decision inside the protected transaction, bound to the same actor/workspace/action/input hash and operation ID. Do not return a reusable client boolean or allow the mutation API without this server-side condition. Web console operations need their own equally strong authorization/step-up; do not trust an Android attestation bypass in a web header.
4. Require valid server authorization for every request regardless of device integrity. A perfectly genuine app with a stolen account or malicious administrator can still commit fraud.

### Tiered handling and availability

| Situation | Proposed server decision and user path |
|---|---|
| Normal signed-in, permitted user on supported genuine install | Allow the authorized action; record minimal risk decision. Do not add attestation round trips to every read/animation. |
| Clear app tamper / wrong package or signer / replay or input mismatch | Deny the protected operation, preserve drafts and show official-install/retry/support guidance. No money or role mutation. |
| Root-risk/device integrity failure | Restrict money, membership/owner changes, privileged exports and verification approval; offer a supported-device path and review. Permit safe help and permitted low-risk reads under the normal data policy. A device verdict alone is not a permanent account ban. |
| Google/network/quota unavailable or verdict unevaluated | Distinguish unavailable from malicious. Fail closed for new privilege/financial mutations; show recoverable pending state, preserve drafts and permit approved read-only/support/cancellation paths. Do not cancel or duplicate a provider transaction already in flight. |
| Accessibility app, older device, signing rotation or enterprise distribution | Test actual compatibility and use documented, narrow server policy; do not blanket-ban accessibility services, require the highest integrity tier for all browsing or hide an unrestricted override. |
| Debug/emulator/test user | Separate staging project and signing/test configuration. A debug fail-open flag can never grant production API access or financial privileges. |

Roll out integrity policy in telemetry mode, measure legitimate failure rates, then enforce by action behind audited server configuration. Cap token-verification request rates before expensive Google calls, monitor quotas, and test a service outage. An emergency policy change is time-limited, narrowly scoped and audited; no global money fail-open switch. Keep refund/cancellation and dispute access available through a secure supported channel even when the Android device is restricted.

## 7. Fraud and misuse risk register

Severity reflects potential harm, not a claim that each exploit was demonstrated. Detection should trigger evidence review and a clear appeal; never permanently blacklist someone from a district/device/IP correlation alone.

| ID / severity | Scenario | Prevent / detect / resolve |
|---|---|---|
| F01 Critical | Buyer of a team plan gains global admin or another tenant's records | Server capability + live membership + resource ownership; explicit cross-tenant denial tests for tables, files, exports and jobs; alert on enumeration. |
| F02 Critical | Stolen founder/admin account changes payouts or invites an attacker | MFA, recent-auth challenge, dedicated privileged sessions, independent approval for exceptions, scoped audit and revocation. |
| F03 High | Fake engineer, copied certificates or expired skills | Proportionate identity/qualification evidence, scoped reviewer badge/expiry, duplicate-document risk review and human appeal; no unverified high-risk job acceptance. |
| F04 High | Fake hospital or manager takes over an existing facility | Independent contact/authority verification, controlled invitation/owner transfer, duplicate facility review; never auto-trust a copied logo/domain. |
| F05 High | Fake organisation recruits engineers or steals independent work | Verified organisation, explicit membership consent and permissions, no inherited access to private history; complaint/block/leave workflow preserving disputes and legal records. |
| F06 High | Job, bid, rating or completion collusion | Immutable actor/timestamp/revision history, one accepted quote, real authorized hospital sign-off, only eligible completed-job reviews, disputeable evidence and velocity/relationship signals. Team lead cannot act as the hospital approver. |
| F07 High | Inflated parts, altered quotation or repeated emergency charges | Versioned itemised quote, hospital approval before cost increase, caps/limits and reasoned exceptions, receipts and dispute path; no silent amount/SLA fallback. |
| F08 High | Reused/photoshopped evidence, wrong equipment or patient images | Job/equipment-linked uploads and server timestamps, duplicate evidence signals, private review and independent hospital confirmation; images and check-in codes are evidence, not definitive presence proof. No GPS mandate. |
| F09 Critical | Replayed checkout, webhook race, fake screenshot or wrong beneficiary causes double payment | Server/provider verification, monotonic ledger/attempt ownership, idempotency and reconciliation, refund-to-original default, beneficiary revision/step-up. |
| F10 High | Trial farming, stolen cards or refund cycling | Quotas, provider risk/KYC, verified organisation history, purchase/workspace binding, safe entitlement revocation and case review; preserve evidence and valid existing work. |
| F11 High | Abusive invitations, messages, OTPs, scraping or expensive exports | Per-actor/tenant/network budgets, pagination, attachment/export limits, queue fairness, report/block controls, verified initiation and escalating challenges. |
| F12 High | Removed engineer or old login sees private notifications/outbox/export | Session/workspace generations, membership recheck, private owner-filtered storage, scoped FCM tags, prompt subscription teardown and short-lived downloads. |
| F13 High | Rooted/modified clone claims clean device or calls API directly | Server-bound integrity at sensitive operation plus normal authorization, replay controls, supported-install remediation and abuse monitoring. |
| F14 High | Malicious upload, compressed image bomb, public file or formula-injection export | Size/pixel/MIME limits, safe decode/re-encode, private buckets, quarantine/scanning as needed, trusted filename/URL handling and spreadsheet-safe output. |
| F15 High | Legitimate user falsely flagged or cannot finish urgent service | Explain actionable restriction, preserve evidence/drafts, support/appeal, separate provider outage from refusal, allow only documented safe existing-work actions; do not silently erase records. |
| F16 High | Admin hides fraud by editing/deleting audit or other member history | Append-only server events, restrictive retention/deletion privileges, tamper evidence, independent backups and tested restore; legal holds are scoped and reviewed. |

Medical-equipment work must expose competence/scope and repair status honestly. An app, payment or verification badge does not certify equipment safe for clinical use. Require the agreed equipment-specific service checklist and authorized hospital acceptance; never encourage use of an unsafe device pending repair or allow the engineer to override that acceptance.

## 8. Privacy, retention and operational resilience

Collect equipment/service information, not patient charts, faces, IDs or clinical records. Capture/upload screens warn against patient information and offer crop/redact/replacement. Do not assume automated redaction always succeeds. Private signed URLs are short-lived and issued only after fresh authorization; removing a member cannot leave indefinitely accessible media/export links. Do not send sensitive job evidence in notification bodies, crash reports or analytics.

Avoid collecting national identity documents merely to create an account. When a justified verification/provider process requires them, collect the minimum via a suitable provider flow where possible, separate access from operational staff, redact/mask appropriately and keep verification status/reference rather than unnecessary copies. No patient-data demonstration fixtures or production dumps in tests.

Before live release, publish a data inventory and retention schedule with purpose, permitted reader, lawful handling basis, expiry/deletion job, backup expiry and hold exceptions for each class: contact/profile, verification documents, job evidence/chat, security audit, payment/tax ledger, support cases and analytics. Proposed operational TTLs such as short-lived challenges and expiring invites are engineering defaults, not a legal retention opinion. Security/tax/dispute records may need specific durations; do not promise immediate deletion of every record or retain everything forever. Audit records contain actor/workspace IDs, action, object ID, timestamp, reason and result; omit secrets, full tokens, raw payment instruments and unnecessary message/evidence contents.

Support understandable notices, consent where required, contact correction, access/export, deletion requests and a grievance route. Record the version of accepted terms/privacy notice without treating one checkbox as consent to unrelated uses. Keep marketing separate from essential service communication. Restrict live workforce accounts to adults for the initial product policy; under-age/student access requires a later explicit design rather than accidental child-data collection.

The notified DPDP Rules 2025 have phased commencement; the official Gazette includes immediate, one-year and eighteen-month groups. Review the current Act commencement notifications, Rules/corrigenda and applicability with qualified advice before launch. This engineering plan neither asserts all provisions are already in force nor certifies compliance. [MeitY official Gazette, notification dated 13 November 2025; accessed 19 September 2026](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

Operational controls: segregate development/staging/production credentials, least-privilege CI tokens, immutable dependency/action versions where appropriate, maintained secret/history scan, verified release signing and private Sentry symbol upload. Index authorization filters and cap query/export/file cost per tenant. Isolate slow/retrying provider tasks so one tenant cannot starve others. Add rate/error/payment-reconciliation/security alerts without exposing raw secrets. Exercise restore, key rotation, provider outage, revocation and an incident response drill. Backups alone are not a demonstrated recovery capability.

## 9. Acceptance and denial test matrix

Every denial test has a permitted fresh-control case. Keep tests synthetic/offline where appropriate and use disposable databases/provider sandboxes for integration. Security review must inspect actual SQL/RPC/storage behavior, not only mocked Compose visibility. No test in this matrix has been executed as part of writing this plan.

| Gate | Adversarial / failure cases | Required permitted control and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| SEC-01 tenant isolation | A requests B IDs through lists/details/search/attachments/exports, RPC and realtime; forged workspace header; no workspace | Own workspace works; disposable DB RLS/RPC plus storage/download tests, including service-job scoping. |
| SEC-02 role/entitlement separation | Paid receipt with no membership; engineer crafts admin request; admin forges founder role; stale privileged JWT after removal; cached lease after removal/expiry, clock rollback or reboot | Permitted active member uses its paid feature; server denial and live role/version check, bounded offline reads and online revalidation; no assertion of immediate remote cache erasure. |
| SEC-03 account cleanup | Suspend each real local mutation, then A→B, A→signed-out→A and A→B→A; same payment ID newer revision; late callbacks | Three existing S1 failures pass unchanged, real Room/DataStore boundary tests plus fresh B persistence and ordinary A logout. |

## 10. Delivery sequence and remaining owner decisions

1. Freeze the four-persona/workspace authority model and migration invariants with server-denial tests. Independently prepare pure three-option registration intent and parent/child region draft validation; this must not extend backend `UserRole`, mutate auth/navigation, create membership or grant an entitlement.
2. Complete S1/S3 ownership/recovery in the isolated candidate; preserve source main's existing QA infrastructure and all failing targets.
3. Add membership/tenant backfill/RLS/storage/worker boundaries before exposing organisation admin pages to live data.
4. Build isolated demo and role-specific registration/onboarding using the chosen existing theme; keep payment affordances disabled until the real rail and lifecycle gates are implemented.
5. Fix payment monotonicity/recovery, then implement team subscription with Play-safe billing and verified provider lifecycle. Prove both independently.
6. Implement the bound integrity gate and founder/admin controls, prove protected mutation denials and legitimate supported-device paths, then expand live pilots cautiously.
7. Finish page workflows, fraud/support/privacy tooling, staged load and device/visual/accessibility checks, signed release and incident/recovery gates.

Commercial price, included seats, provider-supported grace configuration, fee/refund terms, specific provider agreement and regulatory applicability need explicit settled terms before live selling. A live trial is deferred. These decisions do not block building the sandbox, capability model, synthetic tests or non-purchasing UI now. No production migration, billing enrolment, credential change, real payment or account verification is authorized merely by this design document.
