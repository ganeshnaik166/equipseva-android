## 1. Keep what exists, change the authority model deliberately

The owner described four personas, not four omnipotent login flags:

| Persona | Customer-facing choice | Workspace and permitted scope |
|---|---|---|
| Biomedical engineer | **Biomedical engineer** — find work, complete service, join a team | Personal professional identity; own solo work and explicitly assigned team jobs. Team membership cannot confer qualification or let a manager impersonate the engineer. |
| Hospital owner/manager | **Hospital admin** — manage equipment, requests and service | Membership in a particular hospital/site, with owner or delegated manager permissions. Being hospital admin never grants authority over another hospital. |
| Engineering organization/team owner | **Engineering team / company** — manage engineers and service operations | A subscribed organization workspace; owner, dispatcher, engineer and optional finance capabilities defined separately. “Full access” means permitted functions inside that organization, not other customers' records, global founder controls, workers' private finances or credentials. |
| EquipSeva founder | **No public registration choice** | Privately provisioned platform authority, protected by MFA/step-up and explicit audited actions. Founder tools remain separate from paid tenant tools. |

One person may manage a team and also be a qualified engineer, or represent more than one facility. Maintain one authenticated person with multiple memberships and an explicit active workspace; do not create duplicate accounts just to switch tasks. Every screen shows the current workspace name and, when relevant, site/job. The server decides current permissions; a local tile, JWT/UI claim, subscription receipt, deep link or restored screen is not admission.

**Current source:** UserRole.kt still defines the two supported user roles plus deferred supplier/manufacturer/logistics enums; admin is intentionally absent. RootSessionHost.kt:63–96 explicitly admits hospital/engineer. MainNavGraph.kt:109–117 has hospital Home/Bookings/Messages/Profile and engineer Home/Jobs/Earnings/Profile. Profile has an existing role-switch implementation. These boundaries require an owned migration, not a new organization enum value followed by the old engineer fallback.

Desired root sequence: auth resolved -> account restrictions -> membership/capability resolution -> selected workspace -> required setup -> appropriate shell. Preserve current tested account-generation fences and first-time bootstrap. Also extend caches, outbox, payment markers, files, realtime channels, push payloads and saved navigation to the workspace and membership generation. Switching workspace must retire old completions as rigorously as account switching.

## 2. Entry, demo, registration and recovery tree

1. **Already signed in:** resolve the existing account and authorized memberships first. Restore only a still-authorized workspace; otherwise show workspace picker or recovery. Do not force returning users through the new signup questions.
2. **Signed out:** Welcome asks “How will you use EquipSeva?” with the three choices above, plus a clear **Already have an account? Sign in** action. Each tile has a short task-based description; the selected item becomes registration intent only. No paid label can grant administrator access.
3. **Engineer:** identity/contact -> state/district and service coverage -> professional evidence/status -> permitted workspace tasks. Keep qualification and payout readiness separate. The current code requires payout setup at root; move it to capability-gated bidding/payment only in a separate reviewed migration, not as a cosmetic Skip button.
4. **Hospital:** identity -> create facility or accept a scoped invitation -> facility authority/business checks -> state/district/site -> permitted bookings. Duplicate facility detection offers a request-to-join/review path; do not silently merge two businesses based on a similar name, email domain, phone or district.
5. **Engineering team:** choose **Try demo** or **Set up organization**. Real setup requires authenticated owner, organization identity/authority, base/service districts and terms; subscription purchase grants only the purchased organization's entitlement after server verification. Staff join by individual invitation/consent and then obtain only their assigned permissions.
6. **New organization during staged implementation:** until the actual isolated demo and tenant foundation exist, do not expose a tile that navigates to hospital/engineer Main or grants a local admin state. A planning preview must say unavailable/coming next; it is not a demo account or live subscription.
7. **Demo:** launch uses **offline client-local fixtures only**, with synthetic seeded organization, engineers, hospitals, jobs and money; permanent “Demo — sample data” label and separate storage namespace. No separately hosted demo/backend is included at launch; that would require a later explicit product/security decision. Fixture repositories have no production credentials/RPCs, FCM registration, emails/invites, uploads, provider checkout, copied real customer data or shared real account. Demo actions simulate status and reset safely. Exit clears demo state; **Create my organization** starts a new real setup, never promotes sample jobs or synthetic people.
8. **Sign in/provider return:** existing email/password and deliberate Google chooser remain. Authentication success leads to root resolution. Retry on network/bootstrap failure retains same-owner intent; cancellation does not select another account. Password reset sent state is neutral; the existing hosted reset page remains part of the journey. Google-only reauthentication must use its actual provider or a supported recovery method.
9. **Membership invitation:** opaque short-lived single-use token, exact organization/inviter/job-role context, verified intended recipient, accept/decline. Existing user signs in; new user creates a personal identity. Token in logs/analytics is forbidden. Expired/revoked/already-used/wrong-recipient and concurrent capacity/permission changes get explicit outcomes. No automatic team enrollment.
10. **Switch or leave:** show own workspaces and unread/attention indicators scoped to each; confirm unsaved work before switching. Team departure cannot destroy personally owned professional identity or rewrite historical job attribution. Open assignments require transfer/closure/escalation workflow with both party rights preserved. Never allow deletion of the last accountable owner without accepted successor or controlled closure.

Back returns to the source auth page before authentication; after authentication root admission owns navigation. Back, rotation, restoration, notification and external provider return must never reveal another workspace or bypass mandatory setup.

## 3. Navigation shells and main workflow branches

| Shell | Primary destinations | Main journey |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital | Home / Bookings / Messages / Profile | Request service -> compare eligible bids -> approve agreed terms -> pay/verify -> follow job -> review evidence/report -> confirm/dispute -> receipt/history |
| Engineer | Home / Jobs / Earnings / Profile; clear Messages shortcut | Resolve eligibility -> find eligible district jobs or assigned team work -> bid/accept explicit assignment -> attendance/evidence -> service/report -> completion -> actual payout status |
| Engineering organization | Overview / Jobs / Team / Organisation; persistent workspace selector | Verify organization/subscription -> invite consenting engineers -> define service coverage -> receive owned work -> assign qualified available member -> supervise progress -> organization ledger/reporting |
| Founder | Operational console with queues and filters, not a public fifth customer tab | Verification/fraud reports -> inspect minimum required evidence -> authorized decision with reason -> immutable audit -> appeal/reconciliation |

The **Organisation** tab groups Customers/sites, service areas, subscription/invoices, organization settings, support and audit according to permission. **Reports** has a clear labeled action on Overview. Billing and settings remain in Organisation; there is no vague More tab or fifth tab. Finance users do not inherit team administration. Small-device navigation keeps four labeled destinations; larger screens may adapt to a rail. Workspace switching is never implemented as a hidden mutation of a global role string.

Hospitals approve a named contracting party and eventual assigned engineer. Organization owners cannot silently move an agreed solo job or money to their company. Assignment substitution requires valid qualified membership, conflict/availability checks, traceable party notification and fresh consent where terms change. The named technician records their own work and signs their own report. Existing bid/escrow/AMC contracts must retain original payee, fee and authorization semantics until organization contracting is implemented end to end.

No ranking promise such as “nearest”, “3x acceptance” or guaranteed instant emergency service without supporting facts. District eligibility indicates an offered service area, not current physical presence or arrival time. Ask for an explicit arrival window and show a clearly labeled estimate.

## 4. India state/district contract; no maps or GPS journey

- Use a reviewed, versioned snapshot from the Government of India [LGD districts dataset](https://data.gov.in/resource/local-government-directory-lgd-districts) and [LGD directory](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/). Select stable state/UT and district codes; keep English and translated display labels separate. This is the chosen data-source plan, not a claim the latest dataset was imported or all current entries were verified.
- Bundle the approved small lookup snapshot for offline selection; server validates hierarchy and accepts an explicit supported dataset version. Keep issued jobs' state/district codes plus human-readable snapshot at creation. Renames, splits, deactivation and mergers do not rewrite historical invoices/jobs. New jobs require an active mapping; ambiguous legacy names go into a confirmation/review queue, never guess a district from a substring.
- State/UT -> district dependent selection, searchable and fully keyboard/TalkBack accessible. Changing state clears any incompatible unsaved district with explanation. If a valid district is missing/outdated, allow a support request or retained draft; do not invent a trusted free-text code. A legacy free-text value remains visible as “needs confirmation” during migration, not silently erased.
- Engineer **base district** is a profile attribute; **service districts** are explicit operational coverage, possibly more than one state. Team coverage must be supported by active qualified members who consent to those assignments. Hospital organization base and individual hospital sites may differ; a job uses the selected site's district.
- Do not obtain GPS coordinates or continuous/background location for discovery, onboarding, attendance or organization monitoring in the target workflow. No district is treated as proof of professional qualification or facility ownership.
- Physical equipment visits still need a private typed street address, building/floor/department and authorized contact. The hospital supplies it deliberately for the booking. Public feeds/profiles show only coarse service area; exact visit instructions are disclosed only to the accepted, currently authorized participant and minimally necessary dispatcher, with recorded purpose/consent. They are absent from public search, push payloads, logs and demo data.
- No GPS collection from photo EXIF or indirect analytics. Sanitize public/upload derivatives while retaining only explicitly authorized evidence under retention policy. Explain any separately retained evidential metadata precisely; a photo timestamp is not trusted proof of presence.
- Directory/job queries paginate by tenant/coverage/status/skills with bounded limits and stable cursors, not “load all India.” Subscription capacity/assigned active members are server-enforced. Cache only coarse lookup data globally; all private records are scope-keyed. No infinite “live” polling or invented cached financial totals.

### Attendance and evidence without GPS

New contract to implement/test: assigned qualified engineer opens “Start visit”; server issues a short-lived one-use challenge tied to job, assignment version, engineer identity and participating hospital workspace. An authorized hospital representative confirms the named engineer/asset at the visit; the server records both identities, challenge consumption, evidence receipt and timestamps atomically. A short code may be entered manually; never reuse a sign-in OTP or collect another user's authentication secret. Resending invalidates prior challenge, rate limits attempts and does not create another attendance event. Hospital and engineer screens explain that this confirms mutual acknowledgement, **not cryptographic proof of physical presence**; collusion remains a fraud risk.

Before/after equipment evidence must be readable and safely staged, with bounded upload/type/size limits, job ownership, immutable submitted evidence references and retry/reconciliation. A local file or queued upload is not “verified attendance” or “completed work.” Staff should avoid faces/patients/records. Keep condition/serial/report evidence and actual counterpart attestations separate; forged metadata does not become verified proof.

If the representative is absent, device fails or connectivity is down: retain a same-owner draft and show **Awaiting confirmation**; no client “force check-in”, financial release or fake arrival proof. Provide authorized alternate hospital representative, later same-job reconciliation, or support/manual review with evidence, reason and independent approval for high-risk overrides. Show who can help and how to leave safely. Emergency handling must not silently bypass payment/dispute safeguards. Offline fallback does not imply the app is an emergency-response service.

Acceptance covers challenge expiry/replay/brute force, wrong job/engineer/hospital, reassignment mid-flow, simultaneous confirmation, suspension/expired membership, A→B and workspace switches, upload/callback lateness, timeout/restart, hospital absent and collusion review. Existing in-progress jobs must retain a supportable transition during rollout; new and legacy policy versions coexist only by explicit server policy.
