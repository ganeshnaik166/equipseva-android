## 2. The 5 Phases

### Phase 1 — Anchor Win & Second Vertical (4 weeks)
**Goal:** Convert the dental anchor (Clove warm intro) into a contracted multi-site rollout, AND open one parallel vertical to de-risk concentration.

| Feature | Effort | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Clove pilot — 5-clinic rollout playbook + on-site engineer cohort | 2w | warm-intro accepted |
| Second-vertical pick: Ophthalmology OR Sterilization (pick from r486 in-scope taxonomy) | 1w | Clove pilot signed |
| Vertical-specific engineer skill matrix + onboarding ladder | 2w | parallel |
| Hospital chain account model (multi-site, role-based RBAC inside one org) | 3w | Clove > 3 sites |
| Per-vertical pricing & SLA preset library | 2w | parallel |

**Why first:** Cash + concentration risk. One anchor + one vertical = the founder's pitch becomes "two repeatable wedges, not one bet."

---

### Phase 2 — Engineer Growth Engine (5 weeks)
**Goal:** From 50 verified engineers to 250, with quality not collapsing.

| Feature | Effort | Lens / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Certification ladder: Bronze / Silver / Gold tied to r503 verified tiers + completed-job count + dispute rate | 2w | scales r503 |
| Supervised training program: junior engineer paired with Gold for first 3 jobs, paid 50/50 split | 3w | quality floor |
| Referral bounty (engineer-to-engineer): ₹2k on first paid job of referee, anti-collusion gated via r498 risk score | 2w | growth |
| Insurance ladder: free PI + accident cover for Gold tier paid out of monthly platform fee | 3w | retention |
| Engineer skill verification — recorded video PR exercise + senior peer review before tier upgrade | 4w | quality |

**Why second:** Without supply, the demand-side wins (Clove + 2nd vertical) saturate the existing engineers and SLAs collapse. Quality-preserving scale is the unlock.

---

### Phase 3 — Financial Surfaces (5 weeks)
**Goal:** New revenue lines + working-capital relief, without diluting platform fee.

| Feature | Effort | Lens / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AMC subscription tier upgrades (Bronze/Silver/Gold) — bundles preventive PM cadence + Code Red priority + parts discount | 3w | AMC is highest-margin |
| Spare-parts marketplace v0 — list certified parts (from r500 bonded suppliers) with shop-and-ship for hospitals | 4w | new line |
| Engineer working-capital loan (NBFC partner): underwrite against 3-month rolling LTV from r499 | 4w | retention |
| Hospital invoice-discounting: instant 90% on GST invoice, NBFC fronts the float | 3w | hospital cash flow |
| Founder cockpit unit-economics panel — CAC/LTV per cohort + per-vertical contribution margin | 2w | investor readiness |

**Why third:** v0.4 trustworthy enough to safely add money complexity. NBFC partner deals start now (lead time 8-12 weeks) so v0.5 ships with credit lines live.

---

### Phase 4 — Compliance & Standards Leveling (4 weeks)
**Goal:** Cross from "self-certified compliant" to "audited compliant" — opens enterprise + government RFPs.

| Feature | Effort | Lens / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ISO 13485 audit prep — gap analysis + SOP authoring + first surveillance audit | 6w (legal track) | unlocks ent contracts |
| NABH partner accreditation — apply as Type-3 service provider | 8w (legal track) | unlocks gov RFPs |
| Cyber-security review (ISO/IEC 27001-lite) — penetration test + third-party audit report | 4w | hospital procurement asks for this |
| CDSCO advisory submission — file the r486 representation letter (draft already in repo) | 2w | legal counsel |
| Insurance carrier accreditation — Tata AIG / Bajaj / ICICI Lombard add EquipSeva to their preferred-vendor list | 6w (legal track) | demand-pull |

**Why fourth:** v0.4 phases 1+3 already make us compliant by design; v0.5 phase 4 makes us *certified* compliant, which is what large hospital procurement requires.

---

### Phase 5 — Investor Readiness & Geo Test (6 weeks)
**Goal:** Series A material + second geo with measurable unit econ.

| Feature | Effort | Lens / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Geo-2 pilot — Bengaluru OR Pune (pick by Clove footprint), 2-month structured rollout | 6w | de-risk geo |
| Investor data room — Notion/Drive structure + per-month financial pack auto-emailed | 2w | parallel |
| Board cadence — monthly investor letter template + quarterly KPI deck (auto-rendered from /investor route) | 1w | governance |
| Cohort regression analysis — does Hyderabad cohort retention pattern repeat in geo-2? | 4w (data) | unit econ truth |
| Public KPI page (sanitized, tokenized) — link given to prospective LPs/customers | 3w | trust signal |

**Why fifth:** Without geo-2, the investor pitch is "Hyderabad bet." With geo-2 working, it becomes "repeatable playbook + ₹X CAC across two markets."

## 3. What we are NOT doing in v0.5

Same kill-list discipline as v0.4. Explicit kills:

- ❌ International (UAE, Singapore, Middle East). Premature.
- ❌ Class C/D imaging / life-support equipment. CDSCO advisory still pending; risk floor.
- ❌ Direct-to-consumer (B2C household equipment). Wrong wedge.
- ❌ White-label / SaaS platform. Forks attention from the network effect.
- ❌ AI diagnostics / equipment health prediction beyond the existing PM cadence (r507). Capital-intensive, regulatory minefield.
- ❌ Hospital-owned engineer (1099 vs W2 equivalent in India). Tax + liability mess; we stay marketplace.
- ❌ Vertically integrated parts manufacturing. We stay marketplace.
- ❌ Native iOS app. Android-first; web for hospital ops.

## 4. Scoring rubric

Same as v0.4. Every shipped item must score:

1. **Reversibility** — can we roll back if it goes sideways?
2. **Concentration risk** — does it deepen Hyderabad / dental / single-engineer dependency, or diversify?
3. **Compliance δ** — does it shift our regulatory posture? (CDSCO, NABH, DPDP, GST, §65B)
4. **Unit economics δ** — contribution margin per job?
5. **Founder time** — < 4 hrs/week ongoing? Else flag.
